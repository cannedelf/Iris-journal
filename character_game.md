## 📋 PHASE ONE — Building (In Progress)

### The Family

**👨🔧 COLIN GREENWOOD — "Col"**
- Born 1964
- Carpenter — works with his HANDS, shows love through BUILDING not words
- Lives in a stone terrace in Saltaire, near Bradford
- Nobody calls him Colin except the school secretary and his mother
- Was 21 when the sister was born, 26 when the boy was born, 28 when Lucia left
- Became a single dad of two at 28
- Never badmouthed Lucia — "she did her best" and meant it
- Never seriously found anyone else — one woman for 3 months, sister hated her, he ended it quietly
- The house is half beautiful (things he built for the kids) and half falling apart (things he was too tired to fix for himself)
- The kitchen cupboard door has been "about to be fixed" for three years
- Shows love through shelves and desks and fixing things, never through words
- Comes to a gig ONCE, stands at the back, doesn't clap, drives home and cries in the car park
- When asked "so what now" after the boy graduates, the boy says "I don't know" and it's the most honest thing either of them has said

**👩🇮🇹 LUCIA GREENWOOD (née ???)**
- Born 1966 in Italy
- Name means LIGHT — she was the light in the stone terrace and then she left and the house went dark
- Col called her "Lou" because he couldn't say Lucia the Italian way
- Left Italy at maybe 17 or 18 for Col
- Had the sister at 19 before she even knew who SHE was
- Had the boy at 24
- Left at 26 — not a villain, not cruel, just a young woman who couldn't breathe in a stone village where nobody spoke Italian and the sky was always grey
- Postnatal depression nobody named because it was the wrong decade
- Felt shameful RELIEF when she left — couldn't tell anyone
- Probably went back to Italy
- Sends LETTERS — not regularly, not enough. Just enough that the boy knows she exists but not enough to feel like she cares
- Looked DIFFERENT in Saltaire — darker hair, warmer skin, expressive hands, the only person wearing RED in a village of stone and grey
- Was always "Dave's Italian wife" not her own name — actually COL'S wife but the village probably called him Dave or whatever
- Italian maiden name TBD
- The boy has her eyes, her colouring, her hands that move when he talks

**👧 SISTER GREENWOOD (name TBD)**
- Born 1985
- Was 7 when Lucia left — old enough to remember mum PROPERLY but too young to understand WHY
- Became a "little mum" overnight — packed lunches, school runs, knowing where the clean towels were before she knew her times tables
- Once said "she was fine before YOU came" — took it back immediately, never mentioned again, never forgotten
- Left Saltaire as an adult but NEVER misses a birthday — chose to leave AND chose to come back every time. That's the difference between her and mum
- Is a MIDWIFE — watches women choose to stay every day. When she sees the "flicker" of "I can't do this" she says "you can" because she knows what happens when someone doesn't
- Complicated about the boy's music — proud but sometimes thinks "that's not how it WAS." He remembers quiet childhood with gentle dad, she remembers making packed lunches at 7 and being terrified when the bills came
- Goes to every gig she can, claps, tells friends "that's my brother" — but sometimes a song hits too close and she goes to the bathroom and just breathes
- Has all the photos of mum. The boy has NONE.
- Lives outside Saltaire — maybe Manchester or Leeds — in her OWN flat where nobody NEEDS her
- Carries pieces of both parents — not fully dad, not fully mum

**👦 THE BOY GREENWOOD (name TBD)**
- Born 1990 in Saltaire, near Bradford
- Was 2 when Lucia left — too young to hold a single memory of her
- Everything he knows about mum is secondhand — dad's silence, sister's fragments, the letters
- Raised by Col and partially by dad's mum (grandma two streets over)
- Indie folk musician — "the kind you feel special for knowing"
- Not stadium famous — 500 vinyl, 50,000 listeners, every single one would fight for him
- Plays venues not arenas, 500 people in a room all crying at the same line
- His songs sound like someone reading your diary back to you
- Writes about hands and silence and kitchens and letters that came but never enough
- Guitar but not PRETTY guitar — you can hear the fingers move on the strings, imperfections left in
- Sometimes a cello that appears once and never comes back
- Voice is not beautiful but SPECIFIC — a bit rough, a bit low, tells you things it hasn't told anyone else
- Went to uni — somewhere northern, studied something practical he wasn't passionate about, got a 2:2
- Came home to Saltaire after uni, dad said "so what now", boy said "I don't know"
- First gig in a pub in Saltaire, Tuesday night, 12 people and a dog, played the song about dad's desk
- Someone recorded him on a phone, shared it, people kept sending it to one specific person — "this reminds me of my dad"
- Tiny label in Leeds found him — two people who release records because they love music, pressed 500 vinyl
- Has his mum's eyes, her colouring, her expressive hands
- The expressiveness, the warmth, the way his body tells the story on stage — the Italian comes out

**👵 GRANDMA GREENWOOD (name TBD)**
- Col's mum, still in Saltaire, two streets over
- Raised the kids half the time — they grew up on her sofa eating her biscuits watching her telly
- Radio always on in her kitchen
- Biscuit tin never empty
- Never says a bad word about Lucia even though she probably wants to
- The WARMTH of the family
- The boy writes songs about her kitchen and she says "I don't know what all the fuss is about, it's just a kitchen" and means "I can't believe he noticed"

**👴👵 LUCIA'S PARENTS (names TBD)**
- Sided with Lucia — understood their daughter in a way Col's side couldn't
- Maybe told her to GO
- Visits to the kids got less over time — seeing them meant seeing what their daughter left
- The boy gets birthday cards with money from people he can't picture
- The sister remembers their house, the wallpaper, the garden, maybe a dog
- One day when the boy is famous enough, one of them comes to a gig, sits at the back, doesn't say hello — the sister spots them and says nothing

### The Place

**🏠 SALTAIRE**
- Near Bradford, West Yorkshire
- UNESCO World Heritage Site
- Victorian model village built by Titus Salt in 1853 for his mill workers
- Stone terraces all the same
- A village built on the idea that working people deserve beautiful things
- Near Bradford where the Crobat's grandma grew up
- Close enough to Leeds for the music scene but separate from it

### The Rules (from the guide)

- Names come LAST — fit the person, not the other way around
- Avoid the mythology trap — details matter but shouldn't become the whole character
- Each relationship needs its own texture
- Characters should surprise — inconsistent, preoccupied, wrong sometimes
- Not every scene needs a callback or meaningful ending
- The question: what is happening here that has NOTHING to do with the mythology?
