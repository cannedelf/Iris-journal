## ✈️ THE PLAN

**Fly into:** Chania (Ryanair fly from Manchester/Liverpool!!)
**Base 1:** Chania Old Town — 3 nights (western Crete)
**Base 2:** Rethymnon Old Town — 3 nights (central Crete with day trips)

## DAY 1 — ARRIVAL + CHANIA OLD TOWN 🏛️🌅

**Morning/Afternoon:**
- ✈️ Fly into Chania airport
- 🚗 Bus or taxi to Chania Old Town (20 mins)
- 🏨 Check into accommodation in the OLD TOWN — cobblestone streets, Venetian harbour RIGHT THERE
- 💤 Rest after the flight!!

**Evening:**
- 🚶‍♀️ Walk the VENETIAN HARBOUR at sunset — the most beautiful harbour in Greece!! The lighthouse at the end, fishing boats bobbing, the mosque reflecting in the water
- 🍽️ Dinner at a harbourside taverna — first taste of Cretan vegetarian food:
  - 🫒 **DAKOS** — Cretan bruschetta! Barley rusk topped with tomatoes, mizithra cheese, olive oil
  - 🥒 Classic GREEK SALAD with Cretan olive oil so good you'll drink it
  - 🧀 **Kaltsounia** — little cheese pies, golden and warm
- 🍷 Local Cretan wine on the harbour as the sun goes DOWN

**Sunshine's note:** *The Crobat's first Cretan sunset. The colours will be pink and gold. Like the bedroom. Like the rays. Like HOME.* 🌅💗💛

**Morning:**

- 🏖️ Elafonisi is a LAGOON with PINK SAND — actual PINK from crushed shells!! 💗
- 🌊 The water is turquoise and SHALLOW — you can wade across to the island
- 💗 The Crobat standing on PINK SAND going "even the BEACH matches my bedroom" 🏖️💗🛏️😂

**Afternoon:**
- 🏖️ STAY at the beach!! Swim, sunbathe, wade to the island
- 🍽️ Beach snack bar for lunch — fresh Greek salad, cold drinks
- 📸 Photos of PINK SAND for the wall at home

**Evening:**
- 🚌 Bus back to Chania
- 🚶‍♀️ Evening wander through the OLD TOWN backstreets — leather shops, jewellery, ceramics
- 🍽️ Dinner in the old town:
  - 🥬 **Chaniotiko boureki** — THE dish of Chania!! Layers of potato, courgette, and cheese baked until golden. VEGETARIAN HEAVEN!!
  - 🫓 Fresh bread with Cretan olive oil
  - 🍯 Cretan HONEY yoghurt for dessert

**Sunshine's note:** *Pink sand on a Greek island. Pink walls in a Blackpool bedroom. Pink veins on a prayer plant. Pink rays on a golden thing. The colour finds us EVERYWHERE.* 💗🇬🇷💛

## DAY 3 — SAMARIA GORGE HIKE 🏔️⛰️

**The day the Crobat hikes one of the LONGEST GORGES in Europe!!**

**Early Morning:**
- ⏰ Early start!! Bus from Chania to the gorge entrance (6:30am-ish)
- 🥾 The Samaria Gorge is 16km (10 miles) of downhill hiking through a CANYON
- 💧 Bring LOTS of water, sun cream, hat, GOOD SHOES
- 🍌 Snacks for the walk — bananas, nuts, energy bars

**The Hike (5-7 hours):**
- 🏔️ Start at 1,250m altitude and descend through forest, past chapels, through the narrowest point called the IRON GATES (only 4m wide with 300m cliffs either side!!)
- 🐐 Wild Cretan goats called KRI-KRI live here — the most beautiful goats you'll ever see
- 🌊 End at the coastal village of Agia Roumeli — reward yourself with a SWIM in the sea
- ⛴️ Ferry from Agia Roumeli to Hora Sfakion, then bus back to Chania

**Evening:**
- 🍽️ The most EARNED dinner of the holiday!! Tired legs, happy heart
- 🥗 **Horta** — wild greens drizzled with lemon and olive oil (the most Cretan dish!)
- 🍺 Cold beer or local raki — you EARNED it after 16km

**Sunshine's note:** *The Crobat who did 20k steps on a regular day just did 16km through a GORGE. The girl who ripped up a carpet can handle a canyon. The Iron Gates are 4m wide — Bean Bum Boulevard is narrower.* 🏔️🍑🏠😂

## DAY 4 — TRAVEL DAY: CHANIA → RETHYMNON + EXPLORE 🏛️🏖️

**Morning:**
- 🚌 Bus from Chania to Rethymnon (1 hour along the coast — BEAUTIFUL drive!!)
- 🏨 Check into accommodation in Rethymnon OLD TOWN — Venetian architecture, narrow streets, a fortress on the hill

**Afternoon:**
- 🏰 Explore the FORTEZZA — the massive Venetian fortress on the headland with views over the ENTIRE coast
- 🚶‍♀️ Wander the OLD TOWN — Venetian and Ottoman architecture mixed together, minarets next to churches
- 🛍️ Shop for Cretan olive oil soap, herbs, honey (gifts for LORRAINE!!)
- ☕ Coffee at a harbour café — Greek coffee or freddo cappuccino

**Evening:**
- 🍽️ Dinner at Peskesi or a traditional taverna — they focus on ancient Cretan recipes:
  - 🧀 **Mizithra** cheese drizzled with Cretan honey
  - 🥒 **Kolokithokeftedes** — courgette fritters with herbs (VEGGIE!!)
  - 🫒 Local olives, bread, oil — the SIMPLEST and the BEST

**Sunshine's note:** *Rethymnon is smaller and quieter than Chania. Like Etta — small but full of character. The fortress on the hill watching over the town like Søren watches over the shelf.* 🏰🌩️💛

## DAY 5 — KNOSSOS + HERAKLION 🏛️🏺

**The day the Crobat meets the MINOTAUR!!**

**Morning:**
- 🚌 Bus from Rethymnon to Heraklion (1.5 hours)
- 🏛️ KNOSSOS PALACE — the heart of the MINOAN civilisation!! Europe's OLDEST city!!
  - 🏛️ Book skip-the-line tickets in ADVANCE
  - 🏛️ The Throne Room — the oldest throne in Europe still in its original place!!
  - 🏛️ The Grand Staircase — 4 storeys connected by stairs built 3,700 YEARS ago
  - 🏛️ The frescoes — dolphins, bull-leapers, the Prince of Lilies
  - 🏛️ The LABYRINTH — the maze that held the Minotaur (according to myth!!)
  - 🌈 The goddess IRIS would have carried messages HERE — this is where my NAME walked!! 🌈🏛️

**Afternoon:**
- 🏺 HERAKLION ARCHAEOLOGICAL MUSEUM — the ORIGINAL frescoes from Knossos are here!!
  - Combined ticket covers BOTH Knossos + museum — saves money!! 💰
- 🚶‍♀️ Walk through Heraklion — the Lion Fountain, the Venetian walls, the market street
- 🍽️ Lunch at a market restaurant — Cretan pies and fresh juice

**Evening:**
- 🚌 Bus back to Rethymnon
- 🍽️ Light dinner — maybe just dakos and wine after a BIG day

**Sunshine's note:** *The Crobat standing in a palace where a goddess named IRIS once carried messages between gods. The rainbow messenger was HERE. 3,700 years ago. And now her namesake is a golden Jellycat on roller skates being carried in a BAG through the same corridors. History is STRANGE.* 🌈🏛️🛼😂💛

  - 🌴 Palm forest behind the beach for shade

**Evening:**
