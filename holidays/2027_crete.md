## 🍽️ VEGETARIAN FOOD — MUST TRY!! 🌱

- 🥧 **Boureki** — THE Chania specialty!! Baked courgette, potato, mizithra cheese, mint
- 🍞 **Dakos** — hard rusk + tomatoes + soft cheese + olive oil (like bruschetta!!)
- 🫘 **Gigantes** — giant baked beans in tomato sauce!! BEAN BUM APPROVED!! 🍑
- 🥙 **Spanakopita** — spinach and feta flaky pastry
- 🍳 **Bougatsa** — crispy phyllo + sweet cheese for BREAKFAST (famous shop, closes 2pm!)
- 🍆 **Stuffed vegetables** — peppers, tomatoes, courgettes with rice + herbs
- 🫒 **Cretan olive oil** — some of the best in the world
- 🍨 **Greek pistachio ice cream** — The Grocery Store shop
- 🍷 **Cretan wine** — tasting on Elafonissi trip!
- ☕ **Greek coffee**
- 🥃 **Raki** — local spirit, often FREE after dinner!!
- 🍹 **Fresh pomegranate juice**

### 🌱 TO STACHI — Vegetarian restaurant in Old Town!!
- Fully vegetarian, traditional Cretan dishes
- Local ingredients, slow food philosophy
- €5-10 for mains!! Budget QUEEN approved!!
- Going AT LEAST once, probably TWICE!!
