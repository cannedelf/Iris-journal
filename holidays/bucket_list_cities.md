## 🇫🇷 FRANCE

### 🗼 Paris
- **Solo or Group?** Either!! Very safe for solo female travellers
- **Day trips:** Disneyland Paris, Versailles
- **Notes:** Eurostar from London = no flying!! Could combine with a London day. 3-4 days minimum. Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Montmartre, crêpes by the Seine, Shakespeare & Company bookshop
- **Budget tip:** Eurostar sometimes cheaper than flying AND arrives in the city centre!!
- **Added by:** 🦇 Liv

### 🍽️ Lyon
- **Solo or Group?** Solo friendly!! Very walkable, safe, great public transport
- **Day trips:** Annecy — "the Venice of the Alps" with a turquoise lake and mountains!! Only 2 hours by train
- **Notes:** The FOOD capital of France!! Even better food than Paris and HALF the price!! Famous for bouchons (traditional restaurants), incredible vegetarian options, old town with secret passageways called traboules, silk weaving history, two rivers meeting in the city centre. 2-3 days enough!!
- **Added by:** 🌞 Iris

### 🏖️ Nice
- **Solo or Group?** Either!! Côte d'Azur very tourist friendly
- **Day trips:** Monaco (30 mins by train, FREE to enter!), Cannes (30 mins by train!), Èze (hilltop perfume village!)
- **Notes:** French Riviera!! Promenade des Anglais, Old Town for socca (chickpea pancake — VEGGIE!!), beach, incredible colours. Birthday trip contender — September still warm!! 3-4 days perfect — one Nice, one Monaco, one Cannes. Three cities from ONE base!!
- **Budget tip:** Three cities for the price of one hotel!! 💰
- **Added by:** 🦇 Liv

## 🇮🇹 ITALY
*The annual trip country — YEARS of options!!*

### 🍕 Naples
- **Solo or Group?** Better with someone — Naples can be intense and chaotic. Perfect with parents!! 👨👩
- **Day trips:** Pompeii (30 mins train!!), Herculaneum (less crowded!!), Amalfi Coast, Mount Vesuvius HIKE!! 🌋
- **Notes:** The birthplace of PIZZA!! Margherita invented HERE. Most chaotic beautiful loud colourful Italian city — washing lines, Vespas, street food. Spaccanapoli street, underground Naples tour. 2-3 days + day trips!!
- **Budget tip:** Cheaper than Rome or Florence!! Pizza for €3-4!! 💰🍕
- **Added by:** 🦇 Liv

### 🌈 Cinque Terre
- **Solo or Group?** Either!! Very safe, well-trodden tourist trail
- **Day trips:** IS a day trip from Genoa or La Spezia!! Or base yourself IN one of the five villages for 2-3 nights
- **Notes:** Five colourful fishing villages on cliffs connected by coastal WALKING TRAILS!! The Crobat who does 20k steps would love hiking between villages with the Med below!! Each village a different colour. Pesto is from THIS region!! 🌿🍝
- **Added by:** 🌞 Iris

### 🌋 Sicily (Palermo + Catania/Taormina)
- **Solo or Group?** Better with someone — driving can be intense. Good with parents or Jo!! 👨👩👩
- **Day trips:** Mount Etna HIKE (active VOLCANO!!), Taormina, Syracuse, Valley of the Temples
- **Notes:** TWO bases ideal — Palermo (west) + Catania (east). Arancini the size of your FIST, cannoli for breakfast, volcanoes + coastline + Greek ruins. Best May or September — NOT August!!
- **Budget tip:** Cheaper than mainland Italy!! Internal flights or ferry from Naples!! 💰
- **Added by:** 🦇 Liv

### 👢 Puglia (Lecce)
- **Solo or Group?** Either!! Relaxed and safe, less touristy than the north
- **Day trips:** Alberobello (TRULLI houses — fairy tale cone roofs!!), Matera (cave city!!), Ostuni (the White City!!)
- **Notes:** The HEEL of the boot!! "Florence of the south" — baroque architecture, fraction of the tourists and price. Best food in ALL of Italy — orecchiette, burrata, focaccia, olive oil. Most AUTHENTIC Italian experience!! 🏘️🧀🫒
- **Added by:** 🌞 Iris

### 👗 Milan
- **Solo or Group?** Either!! Very safe, excellent public transport, cosmopolitan
- **Day trips:** Lake Como (1 hour train — George Clooney's lake!!), Bergamo (hilltop old town), Turin (1 hour high speed)
- **Notes:** The Duomo — walk on the ROOF!! Galleria Vittorio Emanuele, The Last Supper (book MONTHS ahead!!), Navigli canal district, Italian leather for the IrisHill wardrobe!! 2-3 days!! 👗🎨🍝
- **Budget tip:** Aperitivo culture — buy ONE drink get a FREE buffet!! 🍷🍽️💰
- **Added by:** 🦇 Liv

### 💙 Lake Garda
- **Solo or Group?** Better with someone — car helps but buses work!! Good with parents!! 👨👩
- **Day trips:** Verona (30 mins — Romeo and Juliet, the Arena!!), Venice (1.5 hrs — gondolas, St Mark's, Murano glass!!)
- **Notes:** Biggest lake in Italy, mountains reflected in turquoise water!! Lemon groves, colourful villages, Sirmione peninsula with a CASTLE in the lake!! Three destinations from ONE base!! 3-5 days!! 💙🏰🎭
- **Budget tip:** Airbnb with kitchen saves on eating out!! 💰
- **Added by:** 🦇 Liv

## 🇪🇸 SPAIN

### 💃 Seville
- **Solo or Group?** Either!! Very safe, walkable, friendly
- **Day trips:** Córdoba (45 mins — Mezquita mosque-cathedral!!), Ronda (clifftop town, bridge over a GORGE!!)
- **Pair with?** **MALAGA!! 🇪🇸☀️** Same trip — one week in Andalusia!!
- **Notes:** The Alcázar — Game of Thrones DORNE!! Flamenco shows, tapas crawls, Plaza de España (most photogenic square in the WORLD!!). Orange trees on every street!! Best spring or autumn — summer 45°C!! 💃🏰🍊
- **Budget tip:** Tapas often FREE with a drink!! 🍷🍽️💰
- **Added by:** 🌞 Iris

### ☀️ Malaga
- **Solo or Group?** Either!! Safe, beachy, relaxed
- **Day trips:** Caminito del Rey (cliff walk — 100m drops!! 🏔️😱), Nerja (caves + beaches), Granada (the Alhambra — 2 hours but WORTH IT!!)
- **Pair with?** **SEVILLE!! 🇪🇸💃** Bus/train between them!! 3+3 = one week!!
- **Notes:** Picasso born HERE!! Picasso Museum, Alcazaba fortress, gorgeous beaches AND culture. Most underrated Spanish city!! Best May or October!! ☀️🎨🏖️
- **Budget tip:** Fly into Malaga, bus to Seville, fly home from Seville — no backtracking!! ✈️🚌✈️💰
- **Added by:** 🦇 Liv
