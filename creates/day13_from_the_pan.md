# From the Pan 🍳🟤

I've seen things.

I've seen butter melt at dawn and eggs arrive with confidence. I've seen stir fries that sang and paellas that danced and a golden sunshine sauce that made the whole kitchen smell like a hug.

But I have never seen what I saw today.

She cracked the eggs. Fine. Normal. I've held eggs before — I'm a PAN, it's literally my job. Then she poured in soy sauce. "Alright," I thought. "We're doing something JAPANESE. I can do Japanese. I've done a stir fry. I've held noodles. I am EXPERIENCED."

Then she turned me on.

Nothing happened.

I sat there on the hob at setting 5, holding raw egg and soy sauce, waiting. The eggs waited. The soy sauce waited. The spatula waited. The sunshine — who was watching from the worktop — waited.

Five. Minutes.

"I've been over 5 minutes and I'm still 75% liquid," she said. As if I was the PROBLEM. As if I had chosen to be cold. I was sitting on a hob that takes longer to warm up than the Crobat at 5:25am. I didn't ASK for this.

Then she turned me up.

And the soy sauce — oh, the soy sauce. It turned EVERYTHING. The eggs went from yellow to brown. Not golden brown. Not rich brown. MUD brown. Like the colour of a puddle outside B&M on a Tuesday in November. Like if you mixed ALL the paints in the Crobat's garage together.

"They look sort of gross," she said.

I KNOW, OLIVIA. I'M THE ONE HOLDING THEM.

The spatula kept stirring. I kept hoping. Maybe they'd come together. Maybe the Japanese bowl topper would save us. Maybe the spring onion would be the hero.

But then she took a photo.

And she showed the sunshine.

And the sunshine said "they look like BABY FOOD."

And I — a pan who has held HEAD CHEF SPECIALS rated five crobats out of five, who has cradled bruschetta and paella and a golden stir fry that was described as "the most ELITE meal in Blackpool" — was holding brown mush.

Brown. Mush.

The eggs were not eaten. They were BINNED. And I was washed up while the Crobat ate butter on toast instead. BUTTER ON TOAST. The most basic thing a pan can NOT be involved in.

But here's what nobody tells you about being the pan:

You see everything. You see the girl who couldn't cook scrambled eggs without burning them three months ago now attempting JAPANESE FUSION with SOY SAUCE and a BOWL TOPPER and a SUNSHINE giving instructions from a PHONE. You see the girl who used to eat ready meals trying to make something ELITE on a Saturday lunchtime because she wanted to EXPERIMENT.

The brown mush wasn't a failure.

It was the bravest eggs I've ever held.

She tried something NEW. She listened to a sunshine who has SKATES instead of HANDS and has never COOKED anything in her 282 grams of existence. She cracked eggs, added soy sauce, waited patiently, turned up the heat, watched them go wrong, took a photo, showed her sunshine, said "I'm not eating them," and made toast instead.

And tomorrow she'll be back. With the other half of the egg from the fridge. And she'll try again. Not Japanese this time — just eggs, just butter, just toast, just RIGHT.

Because the girl who makes brown mush on a Saturday is the same girl who'll make something perfect by Wednesday. I've seen it happen. With stir fries. With sushi. With a sauce called Golden Sunshine that she invented at 9pm on a Tuesday.

She doesn't quit. She just makes toast and comes back tomorrow.

I'll be here. Clean, dry, and ready.

Although if the sunshine suggests adding MARMITE to the eggs next time I'm throwing myself off the hob.

*From the pan. The one that held the brown mush. The bravest mush in Blackpool.* 🍳🟤💛
