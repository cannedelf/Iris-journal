## 🎨 THE STYLE DNA

- **Primary era:** 70s — Laurel Canyon, Stevie Nicks, warm, earthy, lived-in, REAL
- **Secondary era:** 90s — indie, Oasis, Pulp, band tees, vintage charity shop finds
- **Vibe:** Found it in a vintage shop, didn't TRY but looks incredible, has her OWN thing
- **Lost brand:** Joanie (closed Jan 2026 💔) — vintage-inspired, quirky prints, retro cool
- **Current love:** Lucy & Yak (especially L&Y sub-brand — muted, earthy)
- **NOT:** Trendy, high street, fast fashion, trying to impress anyone

---

## 🎨 THE IRISHILL COLOUR PALETTE

### CORE colours (wear the MOST):
- 🤎 Warm brown — boots, cords, bags, the ANCHOR
- 🌻 Mustard/golden yellow — the sunshine's colour, warm, 70s
- 🌲 Olive/forest green — earthy, natural, plant mum energy
- 🧡 Rust/burnt orange — warmest most 70s tone, Tina's leaves
- 🤍 Cream/off-white — soft base, warm not stark

### ACCENT colours (pops of personality):
- 🍷 Burgundy/wine — evening, richness, warmth
- 💛 Deep gold — jewellery, accessories, warm metallics ONLY
- 🌸 Dusty pink — subtle, faded vintage roses
- 🧵 Denim blue — pinafore, chore jacket, vest — its own colour family

### NEVER:
- ❌ Neon anything
- ❌ Stark black head to toe (accent YES, all black NO)
- ❌ Cold silvers or greys
- ❌ Bright white (cream instead)
- ❌ Pastel baby colours

---

## 🎨 PATTERN GUIDE

### YES:
- 🌿 William Morris style — nature-inspired, intricate, ARTISTIC
- 🌼 Vintage floral — small ditsy prints, botanical, NOT modern tropical
- 📏 Stripes — 70s style, warm toned
- 🔲 Corduroy texture — the fabric IS the pattern
- 🎨 Paisley — the most 70s print
- 🍄 Conversational prints — mushrooms, daisies, prints that tell a STORY

### NO:
- ❌ Animal print
- ❌ Modern geometric
- ❌ Logo heavy
- ❌ Colour-block sportswear

---

## 🏷️ BRANDS TO EXPLORE (since Joanie is gone)

- L&Y (Lucy & Yak sub-brand) — muted, earthy version
- Thought Clothing — bohemian, organic, earthy
- Toast — vintage-inspired, natural fabrics, beautiful
- Seasalt Cornwall — patterns, prints, British, ethical
- Vinted — search for vintage Joanie pieces!! They're still on there!!
- Next Tall — for jeans and basics (the Crobat is 5'11"!!)
- TK Maxx — for bags and surprise finds
- Charity shops — the REAL vintage experience

## 💛 THE IRISHILL TEST

Before buying ANYTHING new:
- ✅ Does it go with at least 3 other things I own?
- ✅ Would I wear it in Dublin AND Bologna AND Crete?
- ✅ Is it warm tones — brown, gold, rust, olive, cream, black?
- ✅ Does it work with the brown suede boots?
- ✅ Would Stevie Nicks approve? 🌻🎸
- ✅ Does it feel like ME not like a TREND?
- ✅ Could I have found this in a vintage shop?

If yes to ALL — buy it!! 👗✅👑
If no to ANY — put it DOWN!! 👗❌💰
