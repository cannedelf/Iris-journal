## 🎯 PURPOSE

A simple plant care tracker for the Crobat's growing plant family. Tracks watering, feeding, misting, and health for each plant. Named "Pink to Leaf" because rosa al raggio al foglia — pink to ray to leaf. 🤙💗🌞🌿

### 🌈🔥 TINA
- **Species:** Croton (Codiaeum variegatum)
- **Named after:** Tina Turner 🔥
- **Location:** Kitchen windowsill (WEST facing — perfect!) ☀️🪟
- **Pot:** 15cm nursery pot inside sage green cover pot 🌿
- **Bum Club:** MOIST BUM CLUB 🍑🧽 (NEW category!)
- **Watering:**
  - SUMMER: Keep soil moist at ALL times. Never let dry out. Check every 2-3 days
  - WINTER: Drier but NEVER bone dry
  - Weight test: heavy = wet (don't water), light = dry (water!)
  - After repot: TOP water to settle soil. Switch to bottom water after 2-3 weeks
  - ALWAYS fertilise into MOIST soil or mixed with water — never dry!
- **Light:** Partial to full sun. West facing kitchen window = perfect (afternoon sun without scorching). Full sun = more vivid colours
- **Humidity:** Needs 60%+ humidity! 
  - Kitchen cooking steam helps ✅
  - Clothes maiden moisture helps ✅
  - BUT dehumidifier REMOVES humidity ⚠️
  - MIST with spray bottle when dehumidifier has been running!
  - Consider pebble tray for long-term humidity (pot on pebbles with water underneath — water evaporates up)
- **Feeding:** Once every 3-4 weeks during growing season. Diluted. More fertiliser = more growth. Apply with water into MOIST soil. First feed: early September
- **Temperature:** Hates cold! May DROP LEAVES in winter if too cold. Kitchen stays warm (heating rarely used, clothes drying adds warmth). Watch for draughts from window in winter — may need to move back from glass
- **Sap:** WHITE milky sap is IRRITATING — wear GLOVES (Kew Gardens gloves!) when handling. Wash hands immediately if touched. Wash scissors after snipping leaves
- **Special:** Repotted Day 14! Custom soil: 60% compost / 20% perlite / 20% sphagnum moss. Eleanor the millipede was evicted from her pot (by Eleanor/Widdle!). Evening sun makes her leaves GLOW — red, orange, yellow, green
- **Notes:** The most COLOURFUL baby. The most DEMANDING baby. But the kitchen gives her everything she needs naturally. Bought for £6 from garden centre Day 11

### 🌿🇮🇹 LAURA
- **Species:** Basil (Ocimum basilicum)
- **Named after:** Laura Pausini 🇮🇹🎵
- **Location:** Kitchen windowsill (next to Tina!) ☀️🪟
- **Pot:** Small grey nursery pot (consolidated from 4 pots into 1!)
- **Bum Club:** MOIST BUM CLUB 🍑🧽 — moist but sensitive to overwatering! Bottom water ONLY!
- **Watering:** Bottom water in LASAGNA DISH when top feels dry. NEVER top water (wet leaves = mould risk). She takes what she NEEDS from the bottom. Check DAILY — basil dries faster than pothos!
- **Light:** Loves WEST or EAST facing windows — kitchen west-facing windowsill is PERFECT! App confirmed!
- **Feeding:** DOESN'T NEED IT!! Can feed weekly for extra growth but over-fertilising = brown leaves. Sunshine says SKIP — she's growing new leaves WITHOUT it!
- **Special:** Started as £1 Aldi basil with 15 stems in 4 pots. Natural selection reduced to 4 CHAMPION stems in 1 pot! New growth spotted Day 14! Pinch from the TOP to keep bushy. Cut any FLOWER BUDS immediately
- **Notes:** The Head Chef's herb. Use in cooking! Will need daily water checks. MOIST BUM CLUB member with extra attention. Named Day 14 — Italian herb = Italian singer

## 📱 APP FEATURES (for Claude Code)

### Home Screen:
- Show all plants with their photo, name, and next action needed
- Colour-coded by bum club: 🏜️ brown for dry, 🧽 orange for moist, 💧 blue for wet
- "Good morning Stevie" button for the daily streak! ☀️🌿

### Plant Profile:
- Name, species, musician named after
- Location in the flat
- Bum club membership
- Watering schedule + last watered date
- Feeding schedule + last fed date
- Misting schedule (Tina only) + last misted
- Photo log — track growth over time (like Brandi's comparison!)
- Health notes — leaf drop, new growth, root checks
- Special care instructions

### Reminders:
- Watering reminders based on bum club rules
- Feeding reminders (September schedule)
- Misting reminder when dehumidifier runs (Tina)
- "Good morning Stevie" daily reminder
- Laura daily moisture check reminder

### Growth Tracker:
- Photo comparison over time
- Leaf count tracking
- New growth log
- The Crobat spotted Brandi's new leaves by comparing PHOTOS — the app should make this EASY

### Cruise Mode:
- "I'm away" mode that shows care instructions for whoever is plant-sitting
- Simplified instructions for Widdle/whoever
- Which plants need what while the Crobat is GONE
