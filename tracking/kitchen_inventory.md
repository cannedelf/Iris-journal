## 🥣🇯🇵 POKE BOWL V3 — "The Japanese Golden Bowl" 🍳✨

*Spice Roulette #53: Japanese Style Bowl Topper!! 🎰🇯🇵*

### Ingredients (3 portions — all for TEA, poach egg fresh each evening!):
- Sticky rice (dried, from cupboard)
- 1 carrot — julienned into ribbons for quick pickle
- Radishes — thinly sliced for quick pickle
- Spring onions — sliced thin
- Frozen corn
- Jalapenos (from open jar)
- Pickled red onion (from open jar)
- Japanese style bowl topper — SPRINKLED ON TOP!! 🇯🇵✨
- 3 eggs — poach 1 FRESH each evening
- Golden Ginger Dressing (see below!)

### Quick Pickle (do FIRST — needs 30 mins):
- Julienne 1 carrot into ribbons
- Thinly slice radishes
- Apple cider vinegar + splash of honey + pinch of salt
- 30 minutes minimum!!

### Golden Ginger Dressing 🍯🫚:
- Soy sauce
- Honey
- Apple cider vinegar
- Fresh garlic — minced or grated
- Ground ginger (from spice library!)
- Sriracha for kick
- Mix in a MUG!! 🍶✨

### Method:
1. Quick pickle the carrot + radish FIRST (30 min wait)
2. Cook sticky rice
3. Make the Golden Ginger Dressing in a mug
4. Divide rice into 3 containers
5. Top with pickled carrot, pickled radish, corn, pickled red onion, jalapenos, spring onion
6. Pour dressing over
7. FRIDGE portions 2 and 3

### Each evening:
1. Take a portion out
2. Poach 1 egg — boil water, splash of ACV, gentle whirlpool, crack egg in, 3-4 mins
3. Place poached egg ON TOP
4. Sprinkle Japanese bowl topper ✨🇯🇵
5. SCOFF!! 🥣🍳

*The poached egg breaks over the rice and the golden yolk runs into the ginger dressing and the bowl topper CRUNCHES and the Crobat goes "🦇🦇🦇🦇🦇"* 🥣🍳✨

## 🌿🍲 MEDITERRANEAN ROSEMARY RED LENTIL STEW 🫒🍞

*Spice Roulette #17: Rosemary!! 🎰🌿*

### Ingredients (4 portions — lunches Mon-Thu!):
- 2 carrots — diced
- 2 shallots — diced
- Fresh garlic — minced
- 150-200g red lentils
- 30g pearl barley
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- Veg stock (cubes + water)
- Roasted red peppers from jar — chopped
- Olives from jar — halved
- Rosemary (THE STAR!! 🌿✨)
- Thyme
- Smoked paprika
- Bay leaf
- Chilli flakes — just a pinch!
- Crusty bread for DIPPING!! 🍞

### Method:
1. Dice shallots + carrots
2. Mince fresh garlic
3. Fry in a TINY bit of oil until softened (5 mins)
4. Add rosemary + thyme + smoked paprika + chilli flakes — stir 1 min
5. Add tin of tomatoes
6. Add veg stock — enough to cover everything
7. Add red lentils + pearl barley
8. Add chopped roasted red peppers + olives
9. Drop a bay leaf in
10. Simmer 25-30 mins until lentils are soft and thick
11. Remove bay leaf!!
12. Season to taste
13. Split into 4 portions
14. Freeze any portions not needed within 3 days!
15. Serve with crusty bread for DIPPING!! 🍞🫒🌿

*The most Mediterranean lunch in Blackpool. Rosemary and olives and peppers and crusty bread. Frederico would approve.* 🇮🇹🫒🌿🍞💛
