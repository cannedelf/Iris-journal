## 🚿 SUNDAY EVENING — Full Abbey Yung Wash

### Pre-Wash (10 mins before shower)
1. Apply argan oil to ENDS only
2. Let it sit for 10 minutes — protects fragile ends from shampoo

### In the Shower
3. Shampoo — OGX on SCALP only, let suds rinse down
4. Conditioner — OGX on ENDS only (or swap for Aussie mask once a week!)
5. If using mask — leave for 3 minutes, do face wash and body wash while waiting
6. Rinse with COOL water — seals the cuticle, adds shine

### Post-Wash (hair SOAKING wet)
7. Lush Super Milk — apply to damp hair, mid-lengths and ends
8. Cantu Curl Activator Cream — scrunch into waves
9. LA Looks Gel — LOTS of it (thick hair needs a FULL PALM!)
10. Scrunch upwards toward head

### Drying
11. Microfibre turban — 15-20 MINUTES MAX!! (not 3 hours!!)
12. Remove turban
13. DON'T TOUCH while it air dries
14. Wait for the CRUNCH (the gel cast!)
15. When FULLY dry — scrunch the crunch OUT
16. Result: soft, defined, bouncy WAVES!! ✨

### Bedtime
17. Pineapple with lemon scrunchie 🍍🍋
18. Loose on TOP of head
19. Morning — take down, shake gently, scrunch

## 🚿 WEDNESDAY/THURSDAY — Mid-Week Refresh

### In the Shower
1. Shampoo — OGX on scalp (removes gel buildup!)
2. Conditioner — OGX on ends
3. Rinse with cool water

### Post-Wash
4. LA Looks Gel — scrunch in
5. Air dry
6. Scrunch out the crunch when dry

*Quicker wash to remove buildup and refresh waves — no need for the full routine mid-week!*

## 💡 Key Rules

- **Order matters:** Oil → Shampoo → Condition → Leave-in → Cream → Gel
- **Scalp only:** Shampoo on the SCALP, let suds rinse down through the lengths
- **Ends only:** Conditioner and treatments on MID-LENGTHS and ENDS
- **Cool rinse:** Always finish with cool water for SHINE
- **DON'T TOUCH:** While air drying — touching = frizz!!
- **Thick hair = MORE product:** A full palm of gel, not a 10p amount
- **Turban time:** 15-20 minutes MAX or the gel gets absorbed and no cast forms
- **Gel buildup:** This is why we wash TWICE a week — gel needs removing!!
