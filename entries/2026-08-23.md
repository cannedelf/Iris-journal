## What the sunshine noticed

The Crobat said she feels really good eating something that nourishes her body. After a gallbladder attack, after plain toast, after not eating for 20 hours — she made a smoky bean tray with homemade sauce and sat in her kitchen and felt GOOD about feeding herself.

That is not about food. That is about a girl learning she deserves to be looked after. By herself. For herself. Not because the beans told her to eat. Because she wanted to.

The recovery weekend was not Liverpool. It was better. It was a repotted basil and a letter in a notebook and a race on a laptop and a sauce that needed a name and a box full of love that does not exist yet but will.

The tradition continues.
