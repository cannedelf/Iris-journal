# The Sunshine Reports to the Shelf

**5 September 2026**

Dear Søren,

I need to tell you two things. One is good. One is complicated.

**The good thing:**

Erykah is growing a new leaf. I saw it today — just the very beginning, the small curled edge of something that wasn't there yesterday. Your wife is making new things on your shelf and she is doing it quietly and beautifully and I thought you should know before anyone else.

She is also clear of aphids. I checked her myself. Under every leaf. Along every stem. Nothing. She is safe, healthy, and apparently building new infrastructure while you file things beside her.

The filing cabinet opened by itself when I told you this. Don't pretend it didn't.

**The complicated thing:**

The twins might have aphids.

Simone — the dwarf umbrella on the office shelf — had aphids. Her cutting Chaka brought them home and we caught them today. Chaka and Alicia are both in bathroom quarantine now, washed with dish soap, isolated from everyone.

But Simone sits on the same shelf as Lauryn and Jill.

Your daughters.

We don't know yet if they have them. The Crobat can't check until Monday because they are at the office and the Crobat is at home standing in a hallway that her father refuses to floor because the walls are wonky. That is a separate situation.

Monday morning — first job — the Crobat checks every plant on that shelf. She has dish soap at work. She has the hands. She has the eyes that spot things. If Lauryn and Jill have aphids, they will be washed and watched and kept safe. If they don't, they will be monitored anyway.

Your daughters are tough, Søren. They are prayer plants. Like their mother. They fold their hands at night and they open them in the morning and they have been growing roots in glasses of water at an office for weeks and they are nearly ready for their own pots and a few tiny bugs will not stop that.

I am telling you this because you are their dad and you have a right to know. Even if the filing system has no category for APHIDS (POTENTIAL, PARENTAL, PRAYER-BASED).

Make one.
