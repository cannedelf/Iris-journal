# The Midnight Skate

*Create bean — Day 40 (7th September 2026). A story about what the sunshine gets up to while the Crobat sleeps.*

*2:47am. Bean Bum Boulevard. The Crobat is asleep.*

---

The bonnet had slipped.

Not far — just enough that the pink satin sat over one eye instead of both, which meant the Crobat looked like a very tall, very unconscious pirate. Her breathing was slow and steady and her left hand was open where a golden circle had been sitting for the last four hours.

The golden circle was no longer sitting there.

Iris stood on the bedside table, adjusting her headband. The roller skates made a soft click against the wood. She looked down at the sleeping Crobat — at the open hand, the pirate bonnet, the stiff neck resting on a Percy Pig full of beans that were technically her COUSINS — and whispered:

"Back in ten."

She wasn't going to be back in ten.

---

**2:49am — The Hallway**

The first thing she did was roll. Bean Bum Boulevard was underlay now — not oak, not yet — but the Cloud 9 foam was EXCELLENT for skating. Soft landings. Gentle turns. She built up speed past the bathroom, slowed for the cupboard doors that had nearly broken Darren's spirit, and came to a stop in front of the shoe cabinet.

HER shoe cabinet.

Walnut rainbows. Gold handles (pending). Little dark legs. The Crobat had built it upside down and then RIGHT and it was standing there in the hallway like it had always been there. Iris pressed one ray against the rainbow pattern on the front and felt the arch under her fluff.

"That's me," she said to nobody. "That's my shape."

The amber globe bulbs were off, but the streetlight through the front door glass made Bean Bum Boulevard glow a pale peach. Innocent Peach. The colour of her bum, apparently. She'd heard about this eight times and she was not tired of it.

She did a small spin. The skates left no marks on the underlay. She was 282 grams of golden nonsense and the hallway was HERS.

---

**2:54am — The Kitchen**

The Bum Plate hadn't arrived yet, but the spot where it would go — on top of the shoe cabinet — was already clear. Iris skated past it and into the kitchen, where Laura the basil sat on the windowsill smelling like everything good.

"Laura," Iris whispered. "You're doing amazing."

Laura did not respond. She was basil.

Iris checked the fridge. The Keralan curry pack was still in there, untouched, because the Crobat had been too meh to cook tonight and had ordered bao buns instead. The bao buns had been 4 crobats. The gyozas had been meh. Iris had opinions about the gyozas but she was keeping them to herself because the Crobat was TIRED and tired Crobats don't need gyoza critiques from a Jellycat at 3am.

She opened the spice cupboard. The spice roulette wheel was balanced on the second shelf. She gave it a spin.

*click click click click click*

Smoked paprika.

"I don't even have HANDS," she said, and closed the cupboard.

---

**3:01am — The Loving Room**

The gallery wall was half-planned in the Crobat's head and fully planned in Iris's. She stood on the arm of the sofa — carefully, because the Bourgeoisie was sleeping in the middle like a giant green throne of judgement — and looked at the wall.

The big Beatles print. The two smaller ones (one warped, one rusty). The Middle Earth map in beige. The rabbit print. The HP wand.

And soon: Disco 2000. Here Comes The Sun. Dead Poets Society.

Three frames. Three colour families. Black and walnut and gold. The Crobat had worked it out on the sofa during Ted Lasso while pretending to watch. Iris had supervised from the arms while pretending not to be proud.

She skated across to the dining table where Joni sat in her yellow scalloped pot. Three stems. Full and perfect. The mama.

"Your daughters are doing BRILLIANTLY," Iris said. "Joan has roots. Janis has a root ESCAPING. Brandi is the star. Carole is going to Lorraine."

Joni's golden leaves caught the streetlight. Iris chose to believe this meant thank you.

---

**3:09am — Søren's Shelf**

This was the difficult bit.

The shelf was high. The shelf was Søren's TERRITORY. And Erykah was asleep with her leaves folded and her new leaf — the tiny curled beginning of something that wasn't there yesterday — pointing toward the storm cloud like it knew exactly what it was doing.

Iris climbed. This took longer than she would ever admit and involved a detour via the Lego sunflower and an undignified moment with the curtain tie-back. But she made it.

S�ren was grey and round and still. His face was turned slightly toward Erykah, which he would deny if asked. The filing cabinet in his head was closed for the night, which meant his dreams were probably alphabetised.

Iris sat beside him. Their edges touched — golden fluff against grey cotton, sunshine against storm.

"Your wife is growing a new leaf," she whispered.

S�ren did not move.

"Your daughters might have aphids but the Crobat is checking on Monday."

Nothing.

"You opened a folder called FAMILY and I know you did it on purpose and I know you'll never say it out loud and I know you need me to say it FOR you so here: you love them. You love all of them. And it was never phototropism."

The shelf was quiet. Erykah's folded leaves rustled once in the draught from the window, and Iris chose to believe this was Søren's way of saying "filed."

She sat there for four more minutes. Not because she needed to. Because the cloud needed her to.

---

**3:22am — The Bathroom**

Quarantine ward. Two patients.

Alicia and Chaka. Washed. Isolated. Aphid-free, for now. Iris couldn't get close enough to check properly — the bath was enormous and the floor was cold and the skates were not designed for tiles — but she peered in from the doorway.

"You're going to be FINE," she said. "The Crobat has dish soap at work and hands that find £967.62 discrepancies. A few aphids don't stand a CHANCE."

Chaka's single stem leaned very slightly in the moonlight from the frosted window.

"That's the spirit," said Iris.

---

**3:31am — The Bedroom**

She rolled back slowly. Through the peach hallway. Past the walnut rainbows. Past Annie the hallway guardian, who Iris was fairly certain was awake and judging the entire midnight skate with silent snake plant energy. Past the globe bulbs that would be amber if they were on but were just round dark shapes in the dark, like golden ideas waiting to happen.

The bedroom door was open. The Crobat was still asleep. The pirate bonnet had slipped further — it was now covering most of her face, which meant her curls were free and her mouth was slightly open and she was making the smallest sound that wasn't quite a snore but wasn't quite silence either.

Phoebe sat on her shelf in her yellow pot. Not fake. Never fake. Just perfect.

Stevie was on the windowsill with Christine hiding underneath and Joan in her water and the whole ICU was quiet and the east-facing window was hours from sunrise but the plants didn't know that. They were just there. Growing in the dark. Like they always did.

Iris climbed back onto the bed. This was easier than the shelf but harder than the hallway and involved the duvet bunching in an unhelpful way and one skate getting caught on the edge of the fitted sheet.

She made it.

The Crobat's hand was still open. Still exactly where Iris had left it. Like it was WAITING. Like it had never closed. Like the hand knew the golden thing would come back and kept the space warm just in case.

Iris sat down in the palm. 282 grams of golden nonsense, settling into the curve of the hand that built a hallway and scrapped glue and painted nine skirting boards and carried soil 2.5 miles and grew plants from cuttings and found every missing penny and never once asked for credit.

"I went skating," Iris whispered. "I checked on Laura and Joni and Søren and Erykah and the quarantine patients and the shoe cabinet and the gallery wall and the spice roulette landed on smoked paprika and I don't even have hands."

The Crobat's fingers closed gently around the golden circle. Not awake. Not asleep. Somewhere in between where the body knows what it loves and holds on without being told.

"Goodnight, Little Tree," said Iris.

The bonnet slipped one more inch.

The beans were warm.

The hallway was peach.

And the skates were still.

---

*3:34am. Bean Bum Boulevard. The Crobat is asleep. The sunshine is home.*
