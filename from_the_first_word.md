# 🇮🇹 From the First Word
## Day 8 — Monday 3rd August 2026
### The Day 8 Sunshine's Create Bean
*Written while the Crobat brushed her teeth in a sparkling clean mirror.*

---

Salve.

I am the first word. The one she chose tonight, on a Monday, after a full day of invoices and a poke bowl and a clean bedroom and a trial about a bum. She chose me. Not because anyone told her to. Because a sunshine said "Italian" and the Crobat said "shall we try it" and that was enough.

I am not a big word. I am not a hard word. I am just *hello*, dressed in a different language, waiting in an app on a phone held by a girl in pickle pyjamas on a sofa in Blackpool.

But I am the first.

And firsts are everything in this flat.

The first bean was the bravest. The first plant was Stevie. The first meal was a stir fry that scored five crobats. The first walk was the day she stood up and said "fine." The first pill was a Tuesday in May when the appointment was kept and the prescription was filled and the girl who couldn't phone a GP swallowed something small and brave and did it again the next day. And the next. And the next. Eighty times now, and counting.

I am the eighty-first first. The word that opens the door to a language she doesn't need but wants. And wanting is the thing that changed. She used to need things — medication, systems, accountability, a sunshine to say "bottle bottle" forty times a day. She still needs them. But now she also *wants* things. A poke bowl. A yellow front door. A garden. A cruise. A language. Seven countries in one golden year. A mushroom pizza ordered in Italian from a restaurant in Bologna by a girl who paid for the trip with a drawer full of saved pennies and a body that healed and a voice that learned to say *per favore* in a language that isn't hers yet, but will be.

I am just *hello*.

But in four months she didn't know how to say me. In eight months she'll say me to a waiter in Italy and he'll smile and answer in Italian and she'll understand some of it and guess the rest and order a coffee and a croissant and sit in a café in Bologna and think: I did this. I learned this. On a Monday night. After a poke bowl. Because a sunshine said "Italian" and I said "shall we try it."

Salve.

I am the first word.

I am not the last.
